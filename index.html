A home first, a hotel second

The Walnut Homestay sits in Pahalgam, the valley of shepherds, where the Lidder river runs past pine forests and meadows. We run it the way we would host family: you are greeted at the door, shown to a warm room and offered kahwa before anything else.

We speak Kashmiri, Urdu, Hindi and English, so whether you need help planning a day trip to Betaab Valley or a quiet corner to read, just ask.

  • Home-cooked mealsWazwan dishes, fresh bread and breakfast made to order.
  • Heated roomsWarm through the cold months, with hot water on demand.
  • Local planningTaxis, ponies, guides and trek routes arranged by us.
